Decoded content

Decoding runs entirely in your browser. Images and camera frames are never uploaded.

GS1 fields (91) to (99) are reserved for company-internal use, so their meaning depends on the brand. On SUL labels, (91) holds the UID, (92) an internal EAN-13 and (93) the SKU.
